# Core Loop

```
Spin for free → keep the good ones, skip the rest for Shards
      ↓
Fight in the Battlegrounds → earn Yen, build a KO streak (+0.5x luck at the arena machine)
      ↓
Shards + Yen upgrade your abilities · Yen buys luck potions
      ↓
Spin with more luck for rarer abilities → fight stronger players → …
```

**Spinning is free and unlimited**, like an RNG game (e.g. Sol's RNG): there's no spin currency. You stand at a spin machine and press SPIN as often as you like, one spin at a time. What you *earn and buy* is **luck**, not spins. *(LIHFOLK: Teardown - Sol's RNG)*

**The hook in one line:** Every spin could give you a rare anime power, and you can use it to fight right away.

## First 5 minutes (new player experience)
1. **Spin right away:** players spawn at the spin machine and can press SPIN within 5 seconds of joining.
2. **Guaranteed "wow" moment:** The very first spin is at least *Rare* with flashy VFX, so new players feel lucky. ✅ Kept. *(LIHFOLK caution: this can teach that every spin is good. Watch for it in playtests, see [[03 Systems]].)*
   - **No shop popups during the tutorial.** Purchases the game offers on its own wait until after it.
3. **Try it on a dummy:** A training dummy right next to spawn lets them see their ability's VFX and damage safely.
4. **Free luck potion:** Give one luck potion to teach that potions exist and make the next spin exciting.
5. **Enter the Battlegrounds:** An arrow or quest points them to the Battlegrounds arena, and they get a reward for their first hit or KO. Spawn protection (2 s shield) stops them getting farmed the moment they walk in.
6. **Show the goal:** They see a "Mythic" or "Secret" ability on a leaderboard or showcase board and think *"I want that."*

## Short loop (seconds–minutes)
- **Spin:** roll, watch the rarity reveal animation, and keep the ability or skip it for **Shards**.
- **Fight:** battlegrounds-style fights: everyone has the same basic moveset (M1 punch combo, block, dash), and your rolled abilities are your special moves on top. Land combos and KOs.
- **Earn:** each KO gives **Yen**. 3 KOs in a row starts a **🔥 KO streak** (+0.5x luck at the arena's own spin machine). Ending someone else's streak pays bonus yen.
- **Upgrade:** Shards + Yen level up your abilities (+4% power per level, up to level 10).
- **Repeat:** spin with more luck, or upgrade, and fight again.

> Every 1–3 minutes the player should get *something*: a new ability, Shards, a yen pop or a KO popup.
> **Why fight instead of just spinning?** Upgrades need yen (from KOs), the best free luck comes from a KO streak, and abilities are only *used* in the Battlegrounds.

## Mid loop (one session, ~20 min)
- **Daily / session quests:** "Get 5 KOs", "Spin 10 times", "Use a Rare ability". Each pays spins or potions.
- **Playtime rewards:** a luck potion or yen at 5, 10, 15 and 20 minutes (helps the 20+ min playtime goal).
- **Pity system:** a guaranteed Epic+ after 50 spins without one, so unlucky kids don't quit. It's saved, so it doesn't reset when you rejoin.
- **Equip slots:** hold 2–3 abilities at once and mix combos. This is the "combining all anime abilities" hook!
- **Ability storage:** a spin result is only kept if you press Keep or Equip, so you choose what fills your 15 slots.

## Long loop (days–weeks: why come back?)
- **Daily login rewards:** streak of spins and potions, with a big reward on day 7 (helps retention).
- **Collection book / Index:** shows every ability, which ones you've found and which are still "???". Completing a rarity tier gives a reward.
- **Upgrade / Awaken abilities:** level up an ability or merge duplicates to unlock stronger VFX or a new move.
- **Limited-time abilities:** new or seasonal abilities each update (gives a reason to return and works well for update logs).
- **Leaderboards:** most KOs, rarest ability, most spins.
- **Group rewards:** join the Roblox group for a free luck potion (helps reach the 100 community members goal).

## Monetization (fits the 100 Robux goal)
- Game passes: **x2 Luck**, **Extra equip slot**, **Quick Spin / Auto Spin**.
- Dev products: luck potions, yen, Server Luck. **No spin packs**, since spinning is free.
- Keep it fair. Free players should still be able to get everything, just slower.
