# Concept

## Pitch
**Elevator pitch:** Spin for flashy anime-inspired powers, mix them into your own combo build, and battle other players in the Battlegrounds.

- **Genre:** RNG ability roller + battlegrounds PvP
- **The hook:** Every spin could give you a rare anime-style power with cool VFX, and you can **combine abilities** from different anime vibes into one fighting style.

## Target players (age, play style)
- **Age:** All ages, mainly kids (around 9–14)
- **Play styles:**
  - **Collectors / gamblers:** love rolling and chasing rare abilities
  - **Fighters:** want to show off their powers in PvP
  - **Show-offs:** want rare abilities, titles and leaderboard spots
- **Device:** Mobile-friendly (most Roblox kids play on phones), simple controls

## What makes it unique?
- **Use what you roll:** abilities aren't just collectibles. You can fight with them right away.
- **Combo builds:** equip 2–3 abilities at once and mix styles (e.g. fire + lightning + teleport).
- **Big VFX reveals:** rarer rolls get bigger reveal animations and effects.
- **Spinning is free and unlimited** (RNG-game style). You can always spin again. What you earn and buy is *luck*.
- **Fair for unlucky players:** a pity system means everyone eventually gets something good.

## Inspirations (genres, vibes, not copies)
- **RNG roller games:** the rolling excitement and rarity reveals
- **Anime fighting games on Roblox:** flashy moves and PvP feel
- **Anime in general:** power fantasy and cool transformations
- Keep it original: anime-*inspired* abilities with our own names and designs, not copied characters or moves.

## Session length goal
- **One session:** 15–30 minutes
- **Return rate:** come back daily for login rewards and quests

## Success looks like…
- **Players:** 1,000 visits in the first month, 50 players online at once at peak
- **Retention:** players come back the next day
- **Playtime:** average session is 20+ minutes
- **Ratings:** 80%+ like ratio
- **Money:** earn 100 Robux from game passes
- **Community:** Roblox group reaches 100 members
- **Personal goal:** release my first game that people actually play 🎉
